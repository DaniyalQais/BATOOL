# Storefront image sources

Stitched editorial portraits come from the local 1536 by 1024 contact sheet
`stitched-editorial.png`, with Gulnaar, Meher, Saba and Noor panels, each
384 by 1024 pixels. Mira reuses the full Meher portrait, and Zeenat reuses
Gulnaar for festive concept styling. Both uses are disclosed in product data.
Their card-only zooms preserve full portrait imagery on product pages.

`amanah-formal-concept.png` and `layal-formal-concept.png` are restored
AI-generated styling images from the project's saved assets. They are now
used for stitched 3-piece velvet and silk formal concepts. They do not verify
fabric composition, separate garment pieces or real product appearance. The
product captions disclose their generated origin. No new imagery was generated.

The optimizer emits 192px and 384px variants for all eight products and three
optional overview previews at 576px, 864px and 1152px. The hero uses distinct Gulnaar, Meher and Noor portraits, not these overviews. Amanah/Layal preserve
their 3:4 ratio; editorial portraits preserve their original 3:8 ratio. All
product cards share a 3:4 frame. Neither featured row repeats a photograph.

Source PNGs stay outside public assets. Builds require no image-generation
service or network access. Imagery and prices remain illustrative.
