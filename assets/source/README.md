# Storefront image sources

Stitched editorial portraits come from the local 1536 by 1024 contact sheet
`stitched-editorial.png`, with Gulnaar, Meher, Saba and Noor panels, each
384 by 1024 pixels. Mira reuses the full Meher portrait, and Zeenat reuses
Gulnaar for festive concept styling. Both uses are disclosed in product data.
Their card-only zooms preserve full portrait imagery on product pages.

Amanah (ID 9) and Layal (ID 10) were removed after visual review found that
their generated images depicted abaya-style garments and did not support the
stitched 3-piece scope. Their source and optimized assets are no longer used.
IDs 9 and 10 remain reserved; do not assign them to other products. Relabeling
these images as stitched formal concepts does not make them suitable replacements.

The six remaining products are illustrative stitched 3-piece concepts, not
verified merchandise. Imagery does not establish exact garment components,
fabric composition, lining, tailoring, or embellishment specifications. Those
details await brand confirmation; reused concept-image disclosures remain visible.

The optimizer emits 192px and 384px variants for all six products and three
optional overview previews at 576px, 864px and 1152px. The hero uses distinct
Gulnaar, Meher and Noor portraits, not these overviews. All retained portraits
preserve their original 3:8 ratio; catalog cards use a 3:4 frame.

Source PNGs stay outside public assets. Optimization is reproducible offline.
Imagery and prices remain illustrative; no new imagery was generated.
